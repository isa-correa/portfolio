Lume-Mood.app
App Concept · Branding · UX/UI
Lume Mood
AI UX/UI Interactive Prototype Claude
About the project

Lume Mood is an app designed to help users discover and connect with the perfect emotional state through personalised recommendations. The intuitive interface combines colour psychology, expressive typography and careful micro-interactions to create a visceral and empathetic experience.

This project went beyond traditional Figma prototyping. Using Claude as a development tool, I built a fully functional HTML/CSS/JavaScript version that lets anyone test the complete app in real time — transforming the prototype into a tangible experience where every interaction, animation and user flow can be evaluated without simulations.

Lume Mood proves that design is not only visual. Every element — from the colour palette to animation timing — is designed to evoke specific sensations. The app works as an emotional mirror, inviting users to explore, identify and cultivate moods that improve their everyday wellbeing.

The-Phone-Pitch.html
Interactive HTML · Strategy · UX/UI
The Phone Pitch
AI UX/UI Interactive Prototype Claude Strategy Front-end
About the project

A strategic proposal to redesign the product comparison experience on the digital channel of a leading mobile company. The brief came with a real problem: users abandoning key pages, support tickets through the roof, and a 63% bounce rate. The answer had to be as visually compelling as it was analytically sound.

Instead of a slide deck, I built the proposal inside a phone. On desktop it unfolds horizontally — like turning your phone to watch a video — and each section is a standalone app with its own visual identity and interaction: animated numbers on scroll, live charts, a KPI framework with five pillars and projected metrics, and a real-time AI chat integrated via API that lets the client ask questions about the proposal during the presentation itself.

Until very recently, a piece like this required a full team: a front-end developer, an interaction designer, someone to glue it all together. Today I can build it alone. Claude acts as my technical partner: it translates design decisions into working code, iterates in seconds, and lets me reach a level of execution that was previously out of my reach as a designer. It's not an assistance tool. It's a real expansion of what I'm capable of.

Piccadilly-Kiosk.live
Environmental Design · Signage · London 2025
Piccadilly Kiosk
Graphic Design Signage Branding Project Management City Sightseeing London
About the project

City Sightseeing Worldwide is the global reference for hop-on hop-off tourist buses, operating in over 130 cities and serving more than 14 million tourists per year.

As the designer for the international department, I provided design services to some of the company's most prominent franchises — London, Dubai and Abu Dhabi, among others. I led the complete design process of the physical kiosk located at the Piccadilly Circus underground entrance, one of the most iconic tourist and commercial landmarks in the world.

My role was end-to-end: from visual conceptualisation to coordination with the London franchise operator, managing review rounds, on-demand changes and constant communication between teams. It wasn't just about producing graphic assets — it was about maintaining brand coherence in an extremely high-visibility physical environment, ensuring every design decision served both the global City Sightseeing identity and the specific demands of the space.
